How it works

  1. Write your requests

    Describe the practical changes that would help you participate.

  2. Review your card

    Read the exact wording, then approve the card when it’s ready.

  3. Share with your organizer

    Use an event code or invitation to request access, then share your approved card.

Only your organizer sees your shared card.

Your draft stays in this tab. Your organizer can see the requests you approve and share. Approved requests may be sent to Firebase AI Logic if your organizer generates a planning proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Can I write a card without signing in?

Yes. You can prepare, approve, and download a card before signing in. Sharing through an event requires sign-in.

Who can see what I write?

Your private draft stays in your tab. Your event organizer can see the requests you approve and share. If they generate a planning proposal, those approved requests may be sent to Firebase AI Logic.
